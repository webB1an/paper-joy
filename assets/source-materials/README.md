This records the site's general licensing statement for these source files; it is not legal advice or a guarantee of title. Check the individual artwork page and provenance before commercial publication. The items in this folder are raw source SVGs only and have **not** been laid out, print-tested, or reviewed as finished activities. Items marked `pd_issue` or `need-review` must be excluded until resolved; none of those markers were observed on the three item pages when checked.

Page excerpts and individual source metadata are recorded in `evidence/openclipart-pages.md`; per-file source URLs and SHA-256 values are in `manifest.json`.
