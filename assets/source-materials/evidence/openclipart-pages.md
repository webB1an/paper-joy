## Site licensing statement

Source: https://openclipart.org/faq

Visible FAQ, “Licensing” section:
- Says all Openclipart clipart may be used for unlimited commercial uses.
- Says each artist releases rights and the site uses the Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Public Domain License.
- Says `pd_issue` is used when provenance/licensing is doubtful and needs investigation.
- Says new-user uploads are flagged `need-review` while awaiting librarian review.
